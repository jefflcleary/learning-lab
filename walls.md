# Walls

A log of every wall you hit. When you get properly stuck — not ten-seconds stuck,
actually stuck — add an entry: four lines, thirty seconds, done. Fill in the last line
whenever you finally get through, even if that's days later.

Why bother? Because right now, "stuck" feels like a verdict. This file is how you find
out it's a temporary condition. Give it a few months and it becomes a list of things
that were impossible — all of which stopped being impossible — written in your own
words, which makes it the one argument you can't dismiss.

Copy the template, newest entry at the top.

```
## <date> — <a few words for what you were doing>

What stopped me:
What I guessed:
What I tried:
What it actually was:
```
